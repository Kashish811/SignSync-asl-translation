Method

How it works

A signer's body is naturally a graph: joints are nodes and bones are edges. Graph convolutions use that structure and ignore the background and clothing.

1

Find the pose

RTMPose locates 133 keypoints in every frame: body, both hands and face.

2

Build four graphs

Body, left hand, right hand and face each get their own skeleton graph.

3

Graph neural network

Spatial graph convolutions mix neighbouring joints; temporal ones mix frames. Features are fused into pose tokens.

4

Write English

An mT5 encoder–decoder turns the pose tokens into a sentence using beam search.

Questions

Good to know

What is ASL?

American Sign Language is the sign language of Deaf communities in the United States and parts of Canada. It is a complete language with its own grammar, not English spelled out with the hands.

Why a graph neural network?

A signer's body is a natural graph: joints are nodes and bones are edges. A graph neural network respects that structure, so it follows how joints move relative to each other and ignores the background, lighting and clothing.

What do BLEU and ROUGE-L mean?

BLEU counts how many words and short phrases of the model's sentence also appear in the reference translation. ROUGE-L measures the longest sequence of words they share in order. Both run from 0 to 100, and sign language translation scores are low for every system because the task is hard.

How accurate is it?

It captures the gist of many sentences and gets some exactly right, but it can also produce fluent English that does not match the signing. Treat its output as a draft, and use an interpreter for anything important.

What happens to a video I upload?

It is written to a temporary file, translated, and deleted right away. It is not stored or shared.
